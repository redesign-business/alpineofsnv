# Dr. Brandyn Herman, DMD

Board Certified Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon

Dr. Brandyn Herman is a Reno native who always had the goal of serving the community that he grew up in. He attended McQueen Highschool and then the University of Nevada Reno, receiving a bachelors of science in biology. He attended dental school at the University of Nevada Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine where he graduated Magna Cum Laude. Dr. Herman then pursued his Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery training at the University of Cincinnati Medical Center. Following completion of his surgical residency Dr. Herman began practicing at the University of Kentucky specializing in trauma, reconstructive surgery of the face and jaw alignment surgery.

​

In 2022 Dr. Herman returned to Reno to start his practice and business Alpine Oral Facial Surgery to offer exceptional care to the residents of Northern Nevada and specifically to those that are underserved. Dr. Herman is a credentialed surgeon at Renown Regional Medical Center which allows him to offer treatment to those that need advanced care. Dr Herman is a member of the American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons, American Dental Association, Nevada Dental Society and the Northern Nevada Dental Society. 

​

Doctor Herman spends his free time enjoying the great outdoor activities that Northern Nevada has to offer. He is an avid hiker, cyclist and runner. During the summer you can find him at Lake Lahontan camping and boating.

# Dental Implants

* * *

## Service Description

Dental Implants allow for the replacement of an individual tooth, or multiple teeth. Restoring functions and esthetic to give you confidence in your smile and the ability to eat the foods you love. Dental implants are composed of three parts, a crown (the visible portion we use to chew food), an abutment (a support post), and a titanium implant. The titanium implant is anchored into the jaw. Over time the bone will heal and grow around the implant, providing a strong foundation for the artificial tooth.

# Orthognathic Jaw Alignment Surgery

* * *

## Service Description

Jaw surgery, also called orthognathic surgery, is a corrective procedure to realign the jaw. Problems with the jaw are either present at birth or caused later in life by injuries or other medical conditions that affect your jaw. At your consultation for orthognathic surgery, Dr. Herman will create a personalized treatment plan with a biomedical engineer to create the best possible esthetic and functional outcomes.

# Bone Grafting

* * *

## Service Description

Bone grafting is the process of placing granulated bone into a site where there is inadequate bone structure. Loss of bone can occur due to gum diseases, injury, or as a result of previous extractions. Due to bone loss some patients may not be eligible for dental implants. Fortunately, today we have the ability to grow bone where it is needed via bone grafting. Bone grafting allows us to not only place implants but provides us with an opportunity to restore function and esthetic appearance. Dr. Herman is familiar with the most advanced surgical grafting techniques so that you can get the smile you want in a faster and predictable setting.

# TMJ Treatment

* * *

## Service Description

The joint responsible for the opening and closing of the mouth is called the Temporomandibular Joint (TMJ). Temporomandibular Joint Disorders are a family of problems related to this joint. TMJ Disorders are not uncommon and are characterized by pain around the ear or in the jaw joints, trouble opening/closing the mouth, clicking sounds when opening/closing the mouth, and headaches. Treatment of TMJ varies between patients. At Alpine Oral Facial Surgery, Dr. Herman will perform a complete oral examination, review and/or request diagnostic scans, and develop a treatment plan for your condition.
